2D Illustration

5th Place — Walang Hanggang Dambana ng Sining (Eternal Shrine of Art)

57th Shell National Student Art Competition (NSAC)

A national award-winning submission for the 57th Shell National Student Art Competition, securing 5th place overall. Walang Hanggang Dambana ng Sining (Eternal Shrine of Art) aligns directly with the 57th Shell NSAC theme "Lika na, Likha pa ng Obra" ("Come on, let's create more art") by celebrating art as a continuous, generational torch pass. Highlighting varied Philippine art forms, the composition depicts an expanding, infinite canvas: the artists who came before filled their frames and built the canvas with colors. Now, a new generation emerges to take up the mantle and create anew.
